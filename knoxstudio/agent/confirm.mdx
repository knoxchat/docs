# Confirm and Auto

Composer well: lightning = **Auto** (“Auto mode: send directly to generation”), shield = **Confirm** (“Confirm mode: review before sending”). **Default is Auto**.

## What Confirm holds

| Surface | Card |
|---|---|
| Video jobs | **Needs approval** / **Ready to send:** with **Approve** / **Cancel**. Composer locked until then. Esc cancels. |
| Editor destructive batches | **Confirm edits** / **Apply** / **Discard** |

Voice sessions **skip** generation confirm while Connected / Listening / Speaking.

There is **no** separate “hold-for-approval if you edit mid-run” mode beyond Confirm + `EditConfirm` cards.

Stay in **Confirm** when you do not want surprise generations or timeline deletes.
