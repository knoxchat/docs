# Presets and quality

## Builtin presets

Screen Tutorial 1080p · Course Master 4K · Web Preview 720p · TikTok / Reels 9:16 · YouTube 1080p · YouTube 4K · Square 1080 · Twitter / X 16:9 · Animated GIF Small · Audio MP3 320 kbps · Audio WAV.

A social canvas hint appears when the project size matches a vertical or square preset. You can **save your own** presets.

## Resolution

640×480, 1280×720, 1920×1080, 3840×2160, 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% of source, Custom. Even dimensions (codecs require it).

## Bitrate and quality

Bitrate is a **cap**. Quality is the main knob.

| Bitrate preset | Approx |
|---|---|
| Auto | Derived from resolution |
| Low | 2 Mbps |
| Medium | 5 Mbps |
| High | 8 Mbps |
| Ultra | 15 Mbps |
| Lossless | 30 Mbps |
| Custom | |

**Quality:** Fastest / Normal / Slowest — maps to VideoToolbox q, x264/x265 CRF, or ProRes 422 LT / 422 HQ / 4444.

**Encoder:** Auto (VideoToolbox on macOS) / Hardware / Software.

Preferences → General holds **export defaults** (format, FPS) for new export dialogs.
