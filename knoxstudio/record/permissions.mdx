# Permissions

macOS prompts the first time a capture or speech surface needs access — not at app launch. **Preferences → Devices** shows Screen and Camera chips and a **Refresh Devices** button.

## What KnoxStudio asks for

| Area | System Settings | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen Recording** | Privacy & Security → Screen Recording | First screen capture / Recording setup |
| **Microphone** | Privacy & Security → Microphone | Mic in a take, or Voice Director |
| **Camera** | Privacy & Security → Camera | Camera in a take |
| **System Audio** | Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording | System-audio loopback (macOS 13+) |

The app ships usage strings (`NSScreenCaptureUsageDescription`, `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`, `NSCameraUsageDescription`). System audio shares the Screen Capture pane.

`Info.plist` also declares `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription` (“dictation in text fields”). **1.7 does not prompt for Speech Recognition.** Voice Director is Knox realtime speech-to-speech over the microphone — not macOS dictation. You will not see a Speech Recognition toggle for this app unless a later build wires the kit recognizer.

## If a source is missing

1. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and enable KnoxStudio for that category.
2. **Quit and reopen** KnoxStudio. Screen Recording in particular often needs a full relaunch.
3. **Preferences → Devices → Refresh Devices**.

Record and export also need **FFmpeg** (bundled). Devices tab shows bundled vs system vs missing.

## What does not need permission

Opening a project, editing the timeline, writing in Screenplay, and exporting already-imported media. SI features need a <Link to="/knoxstudio/sign-in">signed-in account</Link>, which is not a macOS TCC permission.
