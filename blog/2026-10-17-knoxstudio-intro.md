Talk. Create. Make it real. All on your Mac.

KnoxStudio is a native SI studio — a general agent you speak to or type to. Record. Generate video, images, music, and speech. Write the document. Cut it on a real timeline. Export. One app.


Most creative work is split across a messy stack. One app to record. Another to edit. A third for voice and music. A fourth for images. A fifth for documents. And a chat window that cannot touch any of it.

It’s slow. It’s expensive. It’s exhausting.

KnoxStudio replaces that stack. Recording, timeline, audio, writing, generation, realtime voice, and export live in one place — and they talk to each other.

This is not a chat box bolted onto an editor. And it is not a generator that dumps a file and walks away.

You get a real non-linear timeline. Multi-track video and audio. Trim, split, ripple, insert. Keyframes on the canvas. Annotations that explain. Screen recording with cursor and click effects — what you see is what you export.

Writers stay in the same room as the picture. Drop a PDF or a Word file. Draft a screenplay. Send it to Director Mode. The page and the cut finally live together.

The front door is one SI Agent. Sign in with KnoxStudio. That’s enough.

Ask it to generate a video, an image, a score, a voiceover. Ask the Editor to cut in plain language — “Remove the silences.” “Cut to where she says…” “Caption this clip.” It can watch the footage and hear what’s said.

Ask the Writer to draft the brief. Hand Director a screenplay and it plans the production: shot list, continuity, then place the sequence on the timeline — not a pile of disconnected clips.

You stay in charge. Confirm or Auto. Destructive edits wait for your yes.

Voice Director is that same agent — spoken. Realtime speech to speech.

A glass orb on your desktop. You talk. It makes. Explorer, Timeline, Canvas, Screenplay open beside you while you keep talking. Hang up when you’re done — or return to the editor and keep the call.

You never hunt a menu. You direct.

Media lives on your Mac. SI only runs when you choose to send work out.

When it feels right, export. MP4, HEVC, ProRes, WebM, GIF, audio. Up to 4K. Presets for tutorials, courses, the web.

Speak or type. Make it. Shape it. Then share it.

Whether you are teaching a course, shipping a demo, scoring a picture, writing a script, or producing a campaign — the work stays in one place.

Welcome to KnoxStudio.
